# ISE Voice and Copy

- **Headlines:** short, uppercase, declarative, often two beats: "ONE HALL. FOUR EVENT ZONES." · "BUILD YOUR PRESENCE." · "BUILD THE BUSINESS OF SPORT." · "THE GLOBAL SPORTS ECONOMY MEETS INDIA".
- **Hero lines:** PLAY INDIA. / BUILD TOGETHER. / STRONGER TOMORROW.
- **Tagline:** Be a sport. Shape the future.
- **Actions:** verb + arrow, uppercase: ENTER THE EXPO ↓ · EXPLORE ZONE A → · FIND MATCHES → · COMPARE STALL PRODUCTS → · VIEW → · SKIP INTRO →.
- **Kickers:** `NN — TITLE` in mono; meta lines with ` · ` separators (e.g. `Football · B-SGM-017`).
- **Honesty markers:** every sample is labelled **SAMPLE**, **DEMO** or **PROVISIONAL** (e.g. "DATES PROVISIONAL", "DEMO / SAMPLE CONFIGURATION", "Demo assistant · answers use sample Expo data").
- **Bucky:** friendly, brief, always links to the right page.
